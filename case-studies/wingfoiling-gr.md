# Wingfoiling.gr — bilingual spot and content platform

**Role:** Website development by Stathis Bogris  
**Project:** A TestraTech project  
**Live site:** [wingfoiling.gr](https://wingfoiling.gr/)

## The product

Wingfoiling.gr helps riders explore Greek wingfoiling spots and find conditions, local guidance, news, reviews, videos, and events in English and Greek. The public site presents six Attica spot guides and links from Anavyssos to the separate WatermanVision beach camera.

## My work

I built the website's WordPress-based presentation and content experience. The work brought spot guides, conditions, editorial sections, and bilingual navigation into one site. The spot pages and homepage make location information easy to find while keeping the underlying content editable.

## Engineering focus

- Organizing several kinds of content into a coherent navigation and page structure.
- Presenting English and Greek content within the same experience.
- Connecting spot information and conditions to rider-facing pages.
- Keeping the camera experience as a link to a separate product.
